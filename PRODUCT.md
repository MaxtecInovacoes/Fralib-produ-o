# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
FraLib serves local-business operators and FraLib's internal sales pipeline. The primary viewer of generated sites is a cold local lead seeing a public landing page for the first time on mobile or desktop.

## Product Purpose
FraLib finds local leads, validates facts, generates a persuasive public site, deploys it, and hands the lead to WhatsApp outreach. Success means the generated page feels credible, specific, visually memorable, and ready to sell without manual design work.

## Brand Personality
Sharp, cinematic, commercially fearless. The site output should feel like a senior art director shaped it, while the pipeline remains strict about factual claims.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic WordPress templates, bland white institutional layouts, identical card grids, fake services, AI-looking pastel sameness, decorative glassmorphism, and safe corporate pages that lack depth or motion.

## Design Principles
- Facts are sacred; visual direction is allowed to be bold.
- Every generated site needs one strong first-viewport idea.
- Archetype beats niche: mood, pacing, typography, and media must match the business intent.
- Motion should make the page feel alive without hiding content.
- Local trust must remain visible: city, contact, address, reviews, and hours when known.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Generated sites should target readable contrast, semantic HTML, visible contact paths, mobile-first layout, and reduced-motion support. Motion is welcome, but essential content must remain visible without JavaScript.
