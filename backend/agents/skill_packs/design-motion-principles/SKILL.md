# Design Motion Principles Runtime Pack

Use motion as a finishing system, not decoration.

## Principles

- Purpose: motion must guide attention to CTA, proof, location, or offer.
- Timing: hero entrance can be cinematic; repeated section reveals should be subtle and quick.
- Continuity: scroll reveals should feel related across sections, not random.
- Depth: combine media scale, slight vertical drift, and text reveal for layered composition.
- Restraint: no infinite attention-grabbing animations on reading content.

## Red Flags

- All sections animate the same way.
- Cards float without relation to the surrounding composition.
- Background effects distract from phone, address, or WhatsApp CTA.
