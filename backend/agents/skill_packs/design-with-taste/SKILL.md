# Design With Taste Runtime Pack

Design with a point of view. The site should feel chosen, not assembled.

## Taste Rules

- Pick one visual mood and commit: Bold Impact, Trust Authority, Zen Wellness, Modern Tech, or Luxury Editorial.
- Create tension: oversized headline versus quiet body, full-bleed image versus precise text, dense proof versus open whitespace.
- Break pattern every few sections with a change in scale, alignment, media treatment, or background.
- Use typography as the main visual instrument. Display type should be confident; body copy should stay readable and restrained.
- Avoid fake luxury words. Visual polish is allowed; unsupported business claims are not.

## Local Business Fit

- Fitness and events need movement, contrast, and strong photography.
- Health and wellness need warmth, whitespace, calm motion, and soft editorial rhythm.
- Legal, clinic, finance, and consulting need structure, precision, and authority.
- Luxury, real estate, jewelry, beauty, and gastronomy need restraint, materiality, and image-led pacing.
